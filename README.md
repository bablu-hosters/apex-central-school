## 🏫 About Apex Central School

**Apex Central School** is an educational institution located on **Boyapalli Gate Road, Mahabubnagar, Telangana**.

The website is designed to provide a simple and informative digital platform for students, parents, and the wider school community.

It showcases the school's leadership, campus facilities, student achievements, and important information in a modern and accessible format.

About Apex Central School

Established in 2002 — Nurturing Excellence for Over Two Decades

Apex Central School, established in 2002, is a well-regarded educational institution located at Boyapalli Gate Road, Telangana Chowrasta, Mahabubnagar, Telangana. Managed by the MRK Education Foundation and recognized by the Government of Telangana, the school provides education from Nursery to Grade X and is committed to nurturing students through a balanced approach to academic excellence, discipline, moral values, creativity, leadership, and co-curricular development. With a student strength of around 1,500, Apex Central School has been dedicated to shaping young minds for more than two decades in a safe, supportive, and student-friendly environment.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality education in a nurturing environment while encouraging academic excellence, creativity, innovation, critical thinking, and lifelong learning. We focus on developing strong moral values and leadership qualities so that students are well prepared to face future challenges with confidence. Our teaching approach emphasizes concept-based learning, visual learning activities, general knowledge, current affairs, map pointing, science diagrams, formula practice, graphical representation, and other practical learning methods that make education engaging and meaningful.

Modern Facilities

Apex Central School offers a range of modern facilities designed to support the overall development of every student. These include spacious classrooms, interactive smart boards, an AI-enabled computer laboratory, a well-equipped science laboratory, a rich library, spacious washrooms, a large sports ground, safe school transportation, and a clean and green campus. The school also integrates technology into education through AI-enabled computer learning, helping students develop the digital skills required for the future.

Academic Excellence

For students of High School (Classes VI–X), the school follows the Telangana Board curriculum along with an IIT & NEET Foundation Programme from Class VI. Students are also provided opportunities to strengthen their English communication skills, life skills, conceptual understanding, and computer knowledge. Special attention is given to Class X board examination preparation through morning special classes, evening study hours, slip tests, objective tests, chapter-wise tests, crash courses, regular revision sessions, and individual student monitoring to help students achieve their academic goals.

Co-curricular Development

Education at Apex Central School extends beyond the classroom. Students are encouraged to participate in various co-curricular activities such as essay writing, seminars, spell bee competitions, letter writing, grammar activities, quizzes, and other activities that develop confidence, communication skills, creativity, and leadership qualities. Through a combination of academics, technology, sports, activities, and value-based education, the school strives to provide holistic development for every child.

Leadership & Staff

The school is supported by a dedicated team of highly qualified and trained educators and staff. The institution has 74 teaching staff, along with 11 non-teaching staff, 11 ayyammas, and 4 watchmen, working together to maintain a positive and supportive learning environment. The school leadership includes Correspondent Mrs. K. Shiva Jyothi, Principal (High School) Mr. Parvez Ansari Md, Principal (Primary) Mr. K. Bheema Lingeshwar Rao, Vice Principal Mr. B. Raghavendra Kumar, and Administrative In-charge Mr. Jamanna Raju.

Why Choose Apex Central School?

With over 20 years of educational experience, experienced faculty, strong academic focus, modern teaching methodologies, IIT & NEET foundation support, AI-enabled computer education, excellent sports and co-curricular facilities, safe transportation, and a commitment to the holistic development of every child, Apex Central School strives to create confident, responsible, knowledgeable, and future-ready students.
